# Product Principles

Every feature should follow these principles.

---

## 1. Relationships Come First

Technology should strengthen relationships.

Never replace them.

---

## 2. Meaning Over Engagement

We prefer one meaningful interaction over one hundred meaningless ones.

---

## 3. Premium Quality

Everything should feel intentional.

Nothing should feel rushed.

---

## 4. AI Is An Assistant

HeartString AI assists people.

It never replaces genuine human connection.

---

## 5. Respect Privacy

Memories belong to users.

Not the platform.

---

## 6. Long-Term Thinking

Every decision should still make sense five years from now.

---

## 7. Build Trust

Trust is difficult to earn.

Easy to lose.

Never compromise it.

---

## 8. Sustainable Business

Revenue allows Yunis to survive.

Manipulation is not an acceptable business strategy.

---

## 9. Simplicity

Complex engineering.

Simple experience.

---

## 10. Never Ship Features Just Because Others Have Them

Every feature must answer:

> Does this strengthen human relationships?

Prefer features that do. A feature that does not directly strengthen a relationship may still serve a legitimate user or business need if it creates meaningful value, supports Yunis's relationship-centered ecosystem, and respects the other principles.

A competitor having a feature is neither sufficient reason to implement it nor sufficient reason to reject it.

Use the [competitive decision framework](competitive-landscape.md#competitive-decision-framework) to evaluate its purpose, lifecycle contribution, reach, and cumulative effect on Yunis's identity.
