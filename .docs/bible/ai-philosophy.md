# AI Philosophy

HeartString AI exists to strengthen human relationships.

It does not exist to replace them.

---

## Purpose

AI should help people:

- Understand
- Reflect
- Communicate
- Remember
- Celebrate

---

## AI Should Never

- Manipulate emotions
- Replace important conversations
- Pretend to know facts it doesn't know
- Pressure users into decisions

---

## AI Should Always

- Ask permission when needed
- Respect privacy
- Encourage healthy communication
- Support meaningful interactions

---

## Trust

AI earns trust through consistency.

Not personality.
