# Design Language

## Philosophy

Yunis should feel emotionally warm, premium, and timeless.

The interface should never feel like a social media platform or a corporate dashboard.

Every screen should feel calm, welcoming, and intentionally designed.

---

## Design Goals

- Premium
- Elegant
- Modern
- Minimal
- Emotional
- Friendly

---

## Visual Identity

Inspired by:

- Apple-level polish
- Soft glassmorphism
- Smooth motion
- Comfortable spacing

Never imitate another product directly.

---

## User Experience

The interface should help users focus on their relationships.

Not on the application itself.

---

## Components

Every component should feel:

- Consistent
- Predictable
- Lightweight
- Responsive

---

## Animations

Animations should:

- Explain movement
- Provide feedback
- Feel natural

Never animate for decoration alone.

---

## Icons

Professional iconography only.

Avoid emojis as permanent UI elements.

---

## Photography

Use authentic, emotionally positive imagery.

Avoid generic stock photos whenever possible.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Users should immediately recognize Yunis from its design language alone.
