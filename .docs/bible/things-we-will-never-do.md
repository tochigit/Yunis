# Things We Will Never Do

This document records the principles and boundaries that should guide Yunis as the product, business, technology, and user base evolve.

These principles are not intended to prevent Yunis from adapting.

Yunis may change its features, pricing, business model, distribution strategy, and market position when circumstances require it.

The purpose of this document is to define **how Yunis should evolve**, not to freeze Yunis in its earliest form.

---

## We Will Never

### Deliberately Betray User Trust

We will not knowingly deceive users about how Yunis works, what it collects, how it uses information, or how it makes money.

If an important business or product decision changes, users should be given appropriate transparency.

---

### Treat User Data as an Unlimited Resource

Data may have legitimate business and product value, but Yunis should not treat personal information as something that can be exploited without meaningful consideration for privacy, security, and user expectations.

Where data is used for analytics, personalization, advertising, research, or other legitimate purposes, Yunis should pursue appropriate safeguards, transparency, and applicable legal requirements.

---

### Let Monetization Destroy the Product

Yunis may introduce or change monetization when necessary to sustain the business.

Possible models may include:

- subscriptions
- premium features
- paid experiences
- partnerships
- advertising
- sponsorships
- commerce
- other legitimate revenue models

Revenue is necessary for a sustainable company.

However, monetization should be evaluated against its effect on user trust and product quality.

Making money is not the problem.

Building a business model that makes the product substantially worse for users is.

---

### Use Manipulation as a Substitute for Value

We will not intentionally design dark patterns whose primary purpose is to trick users into actions they would not reasonably choose.

This includes deliberately deceptive:

- cancellation flows
- pricing presentation
- permissions
- notifications
- consent mechanisms
- purchases
- account settings

Yunis should earn continued usage through value rather than deception. We only do these when they are necessary to protect users from harm or to comply with legal requirements.

---

### Optimize for Engagement

Engagement is very useful.

Retention is very useful.

Growth is very useful.

Revenue is very useful.

None of these should automatically become the definition of success.

Yunis should consider whether increased activity represents genuine value rather than simply maximizing time spent inside the application.

---

### Create Artificial Dependency

We should not deliberately manufacture anxiety, guilt, or unnecessary pressure simply to increase retention.

Examples include:

- meaningless streak pressure
- manipulative notifications
- artificial scarcity
- deliberately frustrating cancellation
- intentionally addictive interaction loops
- making users feel guilty for leaving

Useful reminders and encouraging re-engagement are different from psychological manipulation. Although we can encourage users to return, we should not create unnecessary pressure to do so.

---

### Replace Human Relationships With AI

AI may help users:

- communicate
- discover activities
- preserve memories
- create experiences
- reflect
- personalize their Yunis experience
- generate useful content

AI should remain a tool within the product.

It should not be positioned as a replacement for the people users actually care about.

---

### Copy Competitors Without Understanding Why

Competitors should be studied.

Their features, business models, growth strategies, and mistakes can provide useful information.

However, Yunis should not automatically implement something simply because another successful company has it.

Likewise, Yunis should not reject a potentially valuable feature simply because a competitor already has it.

The question should be:

> **Does this create meaningful value for Yunis users and make sense for the business?**

---

### Refuse to Evolve Because of Old Assumptions

Yunis's original vision should guide the company, but it should not prevent the company from adapting.

If users want something different, the market changes, technology changes, or the business requires a new direction, Yunis should be willing to reconsider previous assumptions.

A principle should be strong enough to guide decisions without becoming an excuse to ignore reality.

---

### Sacrifice Security for Convenience

We will not knowingly introduce unnecessary security risks simply because a safer implementation is less convenient.

This includes:

- exposing secret API keys to clients
- storing secrets in source code
- unnecessarily weakening authentication
- ignoring authorization boundaries
- knowingly exposing private user information

Security decisions should account for practical risk, cost, and product requirements.

---

### Ignore Privacy and Applicable Requirements

Yunis should take privacy seriously and comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Privacy should be considered when designing:

- data collection
- storage
- analytics
- advertising
- personalization
- AI features
- sharing
- integrations
- account deletion
- data export

Privacy should be treated as an engineering and product concern, not merely a legal checkbox.

---

### Ship Major Changes Without Understanding Their Consequences

Major features, monetization changes, architectural changes, and significant changes to the user experience should be evaluated before release.

This does not require endless planning.

It means Yunis should understand what it is changing, why it is changing it, and what risks it introduces.

---

### Ignore Accessibility

Accessibility should be considered throughout product development.

Yunis should not knowingly exclude users when reasonable accessibility improvements can be made.

---

### Ignore Scalability

Yunis should not deliberately build systems that cannot reasonably evolve with the product.

Early simplicity is valuable.

Unnecessary over-engineering is not.

The goal is to build systems that can grow without requiring constant architectural reinvention.

---

### Add Complexity Without Value

Yunis should not add complexity merely to appear technologically sophisticated.

Features, systems, and abstractions should have a reason to exist.

Complexity is justified when the value it provides outweighs the cost it introduces.

---

### Forget Why Yunis Exists

Yunis began with a simple idea:

> **Stay connected to what matters.**

Relationships remain an important part of Yunis's identity.

However, Yunis is not required to make every future feature directly strengthen a relationship.

A feature may also exist because it:

- attracts new users
- helps users discover Yunis
- improves retention
- creates useful network effects
- supports a sustainable business
- enables new experiences
- makes the product more enjoyable
- improves accessibility
- enables creators or communities
- creates new opportunities for connection
- solves an important user problem

The important question is not:

> **"Does every feature directly strengthen a relationship?"**

The better question is:

> **"Does this feature create meaningful value while remaining compatible with the kind of product Yunis is trying to become?"**

---

# The Core Principle

Yunis should protect its **identity**, not freeze its **evolution**.

The product may change.

The business model may change.

The technology may change.

The audience may expand.

Features may be added, removed, or completely reimagined.

What should remain is the commitment to building something that provides genuine value to people while maintaining reasonable standards for trust, security, privacy, and responsible product design.

> **Yunis should be principled without being inflexible.**

> **Yunis should be ambitious without becoming careless.**

> **Yunis should grow without forgetting why it exists.**
