Show intrusive advertisements.

---

Sacrifice security for convenience.

---

Release major features without documentation.

---

Ignore accessibility.

Ignore scalability.

---

Complicate the product simply to appear innovative.

---

If a feature violates that purpose, it does not belong in Yunis.
