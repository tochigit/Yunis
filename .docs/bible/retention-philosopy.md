# Retention Philosophy

## Purpose

Users should return because they value their relationships.

Not because they fear losing a streak.

---

## Healthy Retention

Encourage users through:

- Memories
- Milestones
- Challenges
- Anniversaries
- Storybooks
- Bond Movies
- AI Reflections

---

## Unhealthy Retention

Avoid:

- Fear of missing out
- Endless notifications
- Artificial streaks
- Manipulative countdowns
- Excessive daily rewards

---

## Notifications

Notifications should reconnect people.

Not pressure them.

---

## Success

If users leave the app after creating a meaningful memory, that is still a successful session.

The goal is stronger relationships, not maximum screen time.
