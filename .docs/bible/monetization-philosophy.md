# Monetization Philosophy

## Purpose

Yunis exists to build stronger relationships.

Revenue allows the platform to continue serving users for years to come.

Monetization must never come at the expense of user trust.

---

## Principles

Revenue should enhance the experience.

Never manipulate users into spending.

Never lock essential relationship features behind unreasonable paywalls.

---

## Business Model

Yunis earns revenue through:

- Subscriptions
- Hearts
- Marketplace
- Storage Add-ons
- Premium Storybooks
- Premium Bond Movies

These systems should complement each other rather than compete.

---

## Trust

We will never:

- Sell user data
- Sell private conversations
- Use intrusive advertising
- Force purchases

Trust is more valuable than short-term revenue.

---

## Long-Term Thinking

A happy user who stays for years is more valuable than a user pressured into one purchase.
