# Storybook Generation Philosophy

## Purpose

Storybooks transform ordinary moments into lasting memories.

They should feel personal, emotional, and timeless.

---

## Source Material

Storybooks are generated from:

- Memories
- Photos
- Videos
- Voice Notes
- Challenges
- Milestones
- AI Reflections

---

## AI Responsibilities

AI organizes the story.

AI never invents important events.

When context is uncertain, AI should ask the user rather than guessing.

---

## Writing Style

Storybooks should feel:

- Warm
- Personal
- Hopeful
- Authentic

Never overly dramatic.

Never generic.

---

## Permanence

A Storybook should remain meaningful even decades later.

Users should enjoy reading old Storybooks just as much as new ones.
