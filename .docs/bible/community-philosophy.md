# Community Philosophy

## Purpose

The community exists to inspire stronger relationships.

It is not the primary purpose of Yunis.

Relationships always come first.

---

## Principles

Community should feel:

- Safe
- Helpful
- Positive
- Welcoming

---

## Content

Encourage:

- Success stories
- Advice
- Memories
- Challenges
- Celebrations
- Inspiration

Discourage:

- Drama
- Harassment
- Spam
- Toxicity

---

## Privacy

Private Bonds remain private.

Users choose what they share publicly.

Nothing is shared automatically.

---

## Growth

The community should help users discover new ways to strengthen their existing relationships.

It should never distract them from those relationships.
