# Future Vision

## Vision

Yunis should become the world's most trusted platform for preserving and strengthening meaningful relationships.

---

## Five-Year Vision

Users should have years of memories safely preserved.

Opening Yunis should feel like opening a living timeline of their relationships.

---

## Future Features

Potential long-term additions include:

- Physical Storybooks
- Printed Memory Albums
- AI Voice Narration
- AI Video Narration
- Family Trees
- Multi-Generation Bonds
- Shared Journals
- Couple Planning
- Family Planning
- Travel Memories
- Smart Photo Organization
- AI Memory Search
- Collaborative Scrapbooks

---

## Global Expansion

Support:

- More Languages
- Regional Pricing
- Regional Content
- Local Payment Methods
- Accessibility Improvements

---

## AI Evolution

HeartString AI should become better at understanding relationships while remaining transparent and respectful.

New AI providers and models should be adopted without changing the overall user experience.

---

## Platform Evolution

Yunis should eventually exist across:

- Web
- Android
- iPhone
- Tablet
- Desktop

while maintaining one unified experience.

---

## Success

Years from now, users should say:

"This app helped preserve some of the most important memories of my life."

That is the vision every feature should support.
