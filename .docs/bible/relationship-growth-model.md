# Relationship Growth Model

Relationship strength is measured through meaningful activity.

Not addiction mechanics.

---

## Positive Signals

Examples:

- Meaningful conversations
- Memories created
- Challenges completed
- Milestones celebrated
- Time spent together
- Consistency

---

## Signals To Ignore

Do not reward:

- Spam messages
- Artificial streaks
- Rapid clicking
- Repeated meaningless actions

---

## Growth

Relationships should naturally become stronger through genuine interaction.

Users should never feel forced to maintain unrealistic streaks.
