# Heart Economy Rules

## Purpose

Hearts are the premium currency of Yunis.

They should always feel meaningful.

---

## Philosophy

Subscriptions unlock experiences.

Hearts power experiences.

These are separate systems.

---

## Spending Priorities

Hearts are primarily used for:

- HeartString AI
- Storybooks
- Bond Movies
- Marketplace
- Cosmetics
- Gifts
- Events

---

## Free Hearts

Users may occasionally receive Hearts.

Free Hearts should introduce premium experiences, not replace purchases.

---

## Inflation

Avoid creating too many free sources of Hearts.

The economy should remain healthy over many years.

---

## Gifting

Users may send Hearts to others.

This strengthens relationships while keeping value inside the ecosystem.

---

## Future Expansion

Every future premium feature should integrate naturally into the Hearts economy without changing its core principles.
