# Hearts Economy

## Goal

Hearts are Yunis's premium virtual currency.

They power premium experiences throughout the application.

---

# Philosophy

Hearts should feel valuable.

Users should be encouraged to purchase Hearts rather than accumulate unlimited amounts for free.

---

# Uses

Hearts may be used for:

- HeartString AI
- Storybook upgrades
- Bond Movie upgrades
- Profile cosmetics
- Chat cosmetics
- Bond customization
- Community cosmetics
- Marketplace purchases
- Gifts
- Seasonal events
- Raffles
- Premium templates

---

# Earning Hearts

Users may earn limited Hearts through:

- Official events
- Challenges
- Milestones
- Promotions
- Gifts

Free Hearts should never replace purchases.

---

# Purchasing Hearts

Heart packs are sold through in-app purchases.

Pricing is determined by the user's selected country and currency.

---

# Gifting

Users may send Hearts to friends.

Gifted Hearts remain inside the Yunis ecosystem.

---

# Expiration

Purchased Hearts never expire.

Promotional Hearts may expire.

---

# Economy Rules

The Hearts economy should remain balanced.

Inflation should be avoided.

Premium experiences should continue to feel valuable.

---

# Future Expansion

The Hearts system should support future products without redesigning the economy.
