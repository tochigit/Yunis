# Marketplace

## Goal

The Marketplace allows users and creators to discover, purchase, and share premium digital content that enhances the Yunis experience.

---

# Philosophy

The Marketplace should feel curated rather than crowded.

Quality is always more important than quantity.

---

# Marketplace Categories

Examples:

- Storybook Templates
- Bond Movie Templates
- Chat Themes
- Profile Themes
- Profile Frames
- Chat Bubbles
- Stickers
- Icons
- Wallpapers
- Seasonal Collections
- Digital Relationship Guides
- Digital Storybooks
- Creator Packs

---

# Purchases

Items may be purchased using:

- Hearts
- Real Money

Supported payment methods depend on the user's region.

---

# Creator Program

Approved creators may publish products.

Creators can:

- Upload products
- Manage listings
- View sales
- Receive payouts

---

# Product Information

Each product contains:

- Title
- Description
- Category
- Preview Images
- Creator
- Price
- Rating
- Version
- Last Updated

---

# Reviews

Verified buyers may:

- Rate products
- Leave reviews

Creators may respond to reviews.

---

# Moderation

Every submission is reviewed before publication.

Products violating platform rules are rejected or removed.

---

# Future Expansion

Marketplace should support:

- Bundles
- Limited-time sales
- Featured creators
- Gift purchases
- Creator subscriptions
