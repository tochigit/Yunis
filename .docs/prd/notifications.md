# Notifications

## Goal

Deliver timely, relevant, and meaningful notifications without overwhelming users.

Notifications should encourage healthy engagement, not addiction.

---

# Categories

- Bond Activity
- Messages
- Challenges
- Memories
- Storybooks
- Bond Movies
- AI Suggestions
- Marketplace
- Community
- Payments
- Subscriptions
- Security
- System Updates

---

# Smart Notifications

Examples:

- A challenge was completed.
- A new memory was added.
- Your Storybook is ready.
- Your Bond Movie has finished rendering.
- Someone accepted your Bond invitation.
- Subscription renewal reminder.
- Payment successful.
- AI has generated a reflection.

---

# User Controls

Users may customize notifications by category.

Support:

- Push
- Email
- In-app

---

# Quiet Hours

Users may configure quiet hours.

Critical security notifications ignore quiet hours.

---

# Delivery Principles

Notifications should be:

- Useful
- Timely
- Relevant
- Respectful

Avoid unnecessary notifications.
