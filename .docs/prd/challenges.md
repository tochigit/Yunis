# Challenges

## Goal

Encourage members to strengthen their Bond through fun, meaningful, and memorable activities.

Challenges should promote real-life interaction instead of meaningless streaks.

---

# Challenge Types

Examples:

- Daily
- Weekly
- Monthly
- Seasonal
- Anniversary
- Custom

---

# Categories

- Conversation
- Adventure
- Fitness
- Study
- Gaming
- Creativity
- Kindness
- Relationship
- Family
- Self Growth

---

# Creating Challenges

Members can create custom challenges.

Each challenge contains:

- Title
- Description
- Difficulty
- Deadline
- Reward (Optional)
- Proof Required (Optional)

---

# Challenge Proof

When a challenge requires proof, members may upload:

- Photo
- Video
- Voice Note
- Text
- File

Proof is stored with the challenge history.

---

# Challenge Approval

The challenge creator (or eligible members) can:

- Accept
- Reject
- Request another proof

---

# AI Assistance

HeartString AI can:

- Suggest challenges
- Recommend difficulty
- Generate challenge ideas
- Detect repeated challenges
- Celebrate completions

---

# Rewards

Completing challenges may grant:

- Hearts
- Badges
- Bond XP
- Cosmetics
- Event Rewards

---

# Challenge History

Every completed challenge becomes part of the Bond Timeline.

Challenge history may also appear inside:

- Storybooks
- Bond Movies
- Anniversary Recaps

---

# Design Goal

Challenges should encourage meaningful memories, not addiction.
