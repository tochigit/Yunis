# Community

## Goal

Create a positive global space where users can connect, share experiences, and inspire stronger relationships.

Community is optional.

Users should never be forced to participate.

---

# Community Areas

Examples:

- Global Chat
- Relationship Stories
- Tips & Advice
- Challenge Hub
- Creator Showcase
- Community Events
- Giveaways

---

# Global Chat

Users may:

- Send messages
- Share memories
- Share achievements
- Share Bond invites
- Share challenge links

---

# Community Profiles

Community displays:

- Username
- Avatar
- Badges
- Country (Optional)
- Decorations

Private Bond information is never shown.

---

# Moderation

Community includes:

- Reporting
- Blocking
- Muting
- Content filtering
- Human moderation

---

# Community Rewards

Participation may unlock:

- Badges
- Cosmetics
- Hearts (limited)
- Event rewards

---

# Safety

The community should remain welcoming.

Spam, harassment, scams, and harmful content are not tolerated.

---

# Future Expansion

Community should support future additions such as:

- Clubs
- Interest Groups
- Live Events
- Community Challenges
