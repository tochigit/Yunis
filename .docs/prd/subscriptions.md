# Subscriptions

## Goal

Subscriptions provide additional convenience, storage, customization, and premium experiences.

They do not replace Hearts.

Hearts remain the primary currency for premium interactions.

---

# Plans

Free

↓

Plus

↓

Premium

↓

Eternal

---

# Subscription Benefits

Examples include:

- Additional storage
- More Bond slots
- Premium themes
- Higher upload limits
- Advanced Storybooks
- Advanced Bond Movies
- Priority AI processing
- Exclusive cosmetics
- Early feature access

---

# Storage

Subscription storage limits are defined by the business model.

Additional storage may be purchased separately.

---

# Relationship with Hearts

Subscriptions unlock access.

Hearts power usage.

Example:

A subscription may unlock premium Storybooks.

Generating Storybooks still consumes Hearts.

---

# Billing

Billing is handled through supported payment providers.

Pricing is localized based on the user's selected country and currency.

Taxes may be applied where required.

---

# Cancellation

Users retain access until the billing period ends.

After expiration:

- Premium features become unavailable.
- User data is preserved.
- No content is deleted.

---

# Upgrade & Downgrade

Users may change plans at any time.

Benefits update automatically after successful payment.

---

# Future Expansion

Subscription plans should remain flexible enough to introduce new benefits without restructuring the system.
